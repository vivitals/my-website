Private Lessons

Whether you are picking up an instrument for the first time or building on skills you already have, Strike a Chord can help you grow as a musician. Our instructors tailor each lesson to your goals and experience.

Lessons include:

  • One-on-one instruction for your instrument and skill level
  • Practice tips to help you improve between lessons
  • Music reading, rhythm, technique, and performance skills
  • A supportive learning environment for beginners and experienced players

Piano Lessons

Learn melodies, chords, rhythm, and music reading with piano lessons designed around your pace. Beginners can build a strong foundation, while experienced players can work on technique and expression.
